## UI Components
1. **Cyber Trident Header**: Navbar displaying AtlantisJobs Trident icon, chain badge (`BSC Mainnet - 56`), role switcher, and Wagmi Web3 wallet connector.
2. **Abyss Landing Page**: Hero section showcasing 300 USDT BEP20 job post paywall, active job board, and 3-step autonomous pipeline.
3. **Poseidon Recruiter Control**: Recruiter dashboard with 300 USDT wallet transaction modal, candidate match percentage badges, and live AI screening transcripts.
4. **Candidate Hub**: Resume parser converting raw CV text into structured JSON, 10 USDT 30-day candidate boost payment trigger, and 24/7 AI interview room launcher.
5. **24/7 AI Screening Room**: Dynamic 5-question automated screening chat interface generating automated candidate evaluation reports upon completion.
